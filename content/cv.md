## 💻 Research Experience

*All projects supervised by Prof. Zhan Song at the Machine Vision Research Center, SIAT.*

### 🤖 Robot 3D Visual Guidance

**Precise Assembly Based on Hybrid Visual‑Tactile Perception**  
*Feb. 2024 – Oct. 2025*  
- Employed 3D template matching and positional correction for initial visual localization.  
- Developed a non‑contact online tool‑center update method to bridge visual and tactile perception.  
- Proposed a multi‑stage stable assembly strategy combining exponential decay force transition, adaptive admittance control, and coarse‑to‑fine spiral search trajectories.  
- Achieved **>95% success rate** in assembling electronic interfaces with tolerances below **0.1 mm**.

---

**Visual Tracking for Sub‑Millimeter Dynamic Targets**  
*Dec. 2023 – May 2024*  
- Used an SL camera at 30 Hz to dynamically recognise needle and thread via template matching.  
- Adjusted robot pose based on needle‑thread distance for real‑time tracking.  
- Accomplished needle threading with diameter **1 mm** through high‑precision recognition and tracking.  
- **Contributions:** Hand‑eye calibration, background filtering, target recognition.

---

**Automated Laser‑Assisted 3D Measurement of Featureless Surfaces**  
*Oct. 2023 – May 2024*  
- Guided a robotic arm with an SL system to scan large featureless surfaces using actively projected grids; extracted sub‑pixel grid points using cross‑symmetry properties.  
- Applied feature selector and filter‑based optimisation for local registration with noise suppression.  
- Achieved surface stitching with deviation **0.03 mm** using graph‑optimised global registration.

---

**Triaxial Robot Grasping System for Metal Components**  
*May 2023 – Aug 2023*  
- Developed an optimisation‑based hand‑eye calibration method for non‑orthogonal triaxial robots.  
- Trained and deployed U‑Net models to recognise highly reflective metal components in texture maps.  
- Built an automatic grasping system using depth fitting methods to locate components.

---

### 🔦 Structured Light 3D Reconstruction

**Turbid Underwater Structured Light Reconstruction**  
*Mar. 2024 – Dec. 2024*  
- Built a 4‑channel polarised SL system capturing patterns at four polarisation angles.  
- Computed Stokes and degree‑of‑polarisation images to reflect contour information.  
- Developed a boundary enhancement network, enabling 3D reconstruction under turbid water.  
- **Contributions:** System construction, dataset computation methods, network deployment.

---

**Polarised Structured Light System for High‑Reflective Surfaces**  
*Nov. 2022 – Mar. 2023*  
- Designed an SL system with a 4‑channel polarisation camera and a polariser‑equipped projector to capture four sets of patterns with varying brightness.  
- Employed binary time‑coded reconstruction to generate four point clouds.  
- Developed an HDR fusion algorithm to merge point clouds into a unified model.
